WHAT IS KID TALENT MAP?
Kid Talent Map is a scientific tool that provides 21 numbers to help
guide young talents and their parents on the path to success.
Recognizing that proper education and a suitable development
environment are fundamental to shaping a happy child, we have
devoted great efforts to this special product with highly personalized
guidance.
This tool is the result of combining Pythagorean Numerology with Map
for Success. We have analyzed over 200,000 Maps for Success of our
clients in Vietnam and dedicated thousands of hours to researching child
development and parenting methods. Afterwards, we surveyed many
parents to validate our findings and assess practical application.
VALUE OF KID TALENT MAP
Kid Talent Map empowers parents to:
• Understand: The first step to successful parenting is understanding your child. Kid
Talent Map helps you identify their strengths, weaknesses, abilities and values.
• Analyze: Once you comprehend your child, you can determine the right parenting
approach. This allows you to set goals aligned with their values and strengths. The
tool provides a step-by-step process to help them achieve these goals.
• Act: Goals are not enough. You need to create a specific, realistic and achievable
action plan to help them develop on the right track.
• Commit: You should maintain a learning spirit and tailor your parenting style to your
child. Map for Success coaches/practitioners will accompany and support you on your
journey of wise parenting.
